Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production FLOOR TEXTURE ATLAS for a polished top-down pixel-art dungeon game, not a room illustration.
Create a square 2x2 atlas with four equally sized square cells exactly meeting at the horizontal and vertical center. NO margins, NO dividers, NO labels, NO shadows across cells. Each cell is an independent edge-to-edge seamless repeating floor texture viewed straight down with orthographic geometry. All tile edges must match their opposite edge. Lighting uniformly diffuse; low contrast suitable behind bright characters and projectiles. Fine deliberate pixel clusters, hand-crafted high-detail game art, not chunky placeholder pixels, not painterly blur.
Top left: quiet dark blue-grey underwater temple slate paving. Mostly small irregular rectangular stones, a few restrained hairline cracks, very sparse subdued moss in joints. Avoid distinctive repeating patch or central motif. No very large slabs.
Top right: warm muted dark brown shipwreck floor, long parallel horizontal timber planks, thin joints, restrained staggered seams and tiny nails. No braces, metal strips, crates or raised beams.
Bottom left: quiet dark indigo mineral seabed stone floor of small softly angular basalt pieces, sparse tiny blue flecks in joints; walkable flat surface, no coral, plants, rocks, pools or holes.
Bottom right: muted warm grey-beige limestone floor, small diamond/diagonal square tiles, subtle worn edges and small cracks; NOT cream white or bright gold, must contrast with cream-colored boss attack indicators.
Textures must feel calm and even when tiled across a large room. Keep material scale approximately consistent: around 10-14 small stones/planks across each square. Preserve unbroken patterns at cell edges. No characters, UI, emblems, shells, perspective, scenery, vignettes, large dark patches, borders, raised obstacles, glowing details or water.
