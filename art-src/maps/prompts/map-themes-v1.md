# map-themes-v1 — built-in imagegen prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: map environment art direction sheet for an existing top-down 2D pixel-art action roguelite, AI VS NAIWA. This is a visual prototype for map production, not a technical collision diagram.
Primary request: create a polished, cohesive four-theme dungeon environment sheet, with four large room examples in a clean 2 by 2 arrangement. No labels or written text anywhere. Each room is a genuinely different playable floor silhouette with clear walkable routes, not a rectangular floor disguised by decorations.
Style: finely resolved handcrafted pixel art with carefully designed pixel clusters, crisp silhouette edges, restrained texture and rich but controlled shading. Match a cute blue-haired chibi fish maid and expressive detailed frog monsters, although do NOT draw characters. The art should feel finished and elegant at fullscreen 1080p, not primitive chunky prototype tiles, not a blurry painting, not shiny 3D. Orthographic top-down ground with shallow front-facing vertical wall surfaces typical of top-down 2D adventure games; never isometric.
Composition: landscape image, about 1536x1024. Four room examples separated by generous near-black void, equal visual weight and identical art scale. Keep most of each interior calm and open for combat. Walls have designed inner corners and outer corners, coherent thickness, floor-to-wall transitions and readable doorway thresholds. The actual room floor silhouettes must be visibly different.
Top left: sunken stone temple, L-shaped room, dark desaturated blue-grey flagstone flooring, pale blue stone wall caps with restrained old-brass shell details, two doorways with clear thresholds. Include a shallow teal water channel recessed into the stone with carved banks and small stone crossing: the water belongs in a basin, never a blue square decal lying on the floor.
Top right: shipwreck hold, asymmetric stepped long room, desaturated brown plank flooring with broad quiet board patterns, heavy aged timber braces and cool stone/metal footings. Include a few crates and a metal-rimmed red-brown powder barrel integrated into one storage alcove, ample routes around them. Water seeps under a broken hull edge and has a constructed wooden lip; avoid random props scattered in paths.
Bottom left: coral abyss, wide room with one substantial inward notch, dark muted indigo mineral ground and blue-grey rock boundary, restrained coral pink and soft teal sea growth at the perimeter only. Include one small recessed current channel with rock banks and one inset ground spike mechanism: mechanical base frame integrated in the floor, subtly lighter than the ground. No huge saturated hazard rectangle.
Bottom right: milk sanctuary boss chamber, broad clipped-corner room, softly muted warm-cream and dusty gold stone, pale cool grey shadows, four purposeful squat stone pillars with clear bases and dark contact shadows. Include milk-white liquid in shallow sculpted side basins with stepped rims, distinct from walkable floor; leave a spacious middle for boss combat. Do not make the floor near-white or glaring.
Lighting and hierarchy: one consistent upper-left illumination, dark floors calmer than vertical obstacles, small restrained warm lanterns, crisp readable edges. Same material finish and apparent pixel density across all four themes. Floor pattern contrast low enough for red bullets, cream boss cues and colorful monsters to stand out. Corners, pools, terrain and props form one authored environment.
Avoid: text, labels, watermarks, logos, characters, UI, weapons, attack indicators, red telegraph circles, coarse thick outlines, detached water stickers, giant arrows on water, visual noise, bloom, haze, unrealistic smooth gradients, perspective camera, ornate borders around the sheet.
